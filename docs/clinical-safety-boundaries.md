# Clinical Safety Boundaries

Status: mandatory development constraints; clinical language and thresholds remain placeholders until approved.

1. COMPASS is not an emergency service and is not continuously monitored.
2. Urgent patient-facing guidance cannot wait for nurse review.
3. AI does not independently diagnose, prescribe, set emergency conclusions, or update formal ECOG.
4. Alert triggers and urgent instructions are deterministic, versioned, testable, and clinician-approved.
5. Patient answers come only from active clinician-approved knowledge content.
6. Low-confidence or unsupported questions are acknowledged and redirected.
7. AI summaries link to source records and never replace them.
8. Core reporting, storage, alerts, and emergency-access UI remain functional during AI outages.
9. Real patient data is prohibited in development and unapproved environments.
10. Clinical email alerts contain no sensitive details.
11. Every clinically meaningful automated decision retains its inputs, rule/content version, output, and timestamp.
12. Placeholder clinical content must be visibly labeled and impossible to confuse with approved guidance.
